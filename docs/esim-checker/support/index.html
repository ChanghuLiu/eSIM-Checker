Common Questions

Why does the app say "eSIM Not Detected"?
Android does not report eSIM hardware support on your device. Regional variants, firmware, carrier configuration, or device type may affect the result.
Why does the app say "Needs Verification"?
Android reports some eSIM capability, but the eSIM service or another check still requires confirmation.
Can eSIM Checker tell whether my phone is carrier unlocked?
No. eSIM Checker does not claim to automatically detect carrier lock status. Please confirm this with your mobile carrier.
Does eSIM Checker guarantee that an eSIM will work?
No. Final compatibility depends on your exact device model, regional variant, carrier, firmware, and eSIM provider.
Does the app upload my device information?
No. Compatibility checking is performed locally.
